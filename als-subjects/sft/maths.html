Mathematics Notes

Geometry, mensuration, measurement calculations, Pythagoras, trigonometry, functions and descriptive statistics.

Syllabus-aligned study material based on the uploaded NIE Grade 12–13 Science for Technology syllabus. Sinhala explanations and further diagrams, examples and activities are being expanded chapter by chapter.
TOPIC 01

Area, surface area and volume

Calculate areas of square, rectangle, triangle, parallelogram, trapezium and circle. Extend to surface area and volume of cube, cuboid, pyramid, prism, cylinder, sphere and cone.

Key notes

Break compound figures into familiar shapes. For combined solids, identify external surfaces only and avoid counting joined internal faces.

TOPIC 02

Measurement and significant accuracy

Work with physical quantities, SI units, prefixes, least count, zero error, fractional error and percentage error.

Key notes

Convert units before substitution. Record answers to a precision justified by the instrument and measurement.

TOPIC 03

Pythagoras relationship

For a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of squares of the other two sides. The converse tests whether a triangle is right-angled.

Key notes

c² = a² + b². Worked examples should include finding an unknown side and checking a triangle using the converse.

TOPIC 04

Trigonometric ratios

Use degrees and radians; define sine, cosine and tangent up to the second quadrant as covered in the syllabus. Apply ratios to right triangles and practical elevation/depression problems.

Key notes

sin θ = opposite/hypotenuse; cos θ = adjacent/hypotenuse; tan θ = opposite/adjacent. Identity: sin²θ + cos²θ = 1.

TOPIC 05

Arc length, sectors and triangle area

Use radians to calculate arc length and sector area; calculate triangle area when two sides and the included angle are known.

Key notes

Arc length ℓ = rθ; sector area A = ½r²θ (θ in radians); triangle area A = ½ab sin C.

TOPIC 06

Linear and quadratic functions

Represent linear and quadratic relationships on the Cartesian plane. Interpret gradients, intercepts, roots and graph shapes in context.

Key notes

For y = mx + c, m is gradient. For y = ax² + bx + c, the graph is a parabola; identify roots and turning point using methods appropriate to the taught level.

TOPIC 07

Descriptive statistics

Organise data using frequency distributions and graphical displays. Study measures of central tendency and measures of spread for grouped and ungrouped data.

Key notes

Mean, median and mode describe centre. Range, quartiles and interquartile range describe spread. Interpret cumulative frequency and percentage cumulative frequency curves.
