Chemistry Notes

Thermochemistry, reaction rates, polymers, chemical industries, natural product extraction and environmental chemistry.

Syllabus-aligned study material based on the uploaded NIE Grade 12–13 Science for Technology syllabus. Sinhala explanations and further diagrams, examples and activities are being expanded chapter by chapter.
TOPIC 01

Thermochemistry

Define heat, work, energy, system, surroundings and boundary. Distinguish open, closed and isolated systems. Describe physical changes of state at molecular level.

Key notes

Exothermic reactions release heat to surroundings; endothermic reactions absorb heat. Interpret energy-profile diagrams and calculate simple heat changes from experimental acid-base data, stating assumptions.

TOPIC 02

Chemical kinetics

Reaction rate is change in a measurable quantity per unit time. Investigate temperature, concentration, pressure where relevant, surface area and catalysts.

Key notes

Collision theory: particles must collide, have suitable orientation and possess sufficient energy to overcome activation energy. Explain single-step and multi-step reactions and the rate-determining step.

TOPIC 03

Polymers

Study polymer materials and their industrial uses, including relationships between structure and mechanical properties.

Key notes

Organise notes around monomers, polymerisation, properties, processing, uses, recycling and environmental concerns. Distinguish thermoplastics, thermosets and elastomers where covered.

TOPIC 04

Chemical industries in Sri Lanka

Study the syllabus-selected chemical industries, raw materials, main processes, products, uses and environmental considerations.

Key notes

For each industry make a process-flow summary: raw material → key process → product → application → waste/safety/environment.

TOPIC 05

Natural products and extraction

Investigate natural products and methods used to extract them. Link extraction method to the physical/chemical properties of the target product.

Key notes

Compare suitable methods such as pressing, solvent extraction, distillation or steam distillation only where relevant to the specific syllabus examples. Include separation, purification and safe handling.

TOPIC 06

Environmental chemistry

Study natural cycles and the impact of human activity on water, atmosphere and lithosphere; examine pollution indicators and mitigation.

Key notes

Water: pH, conductivity, COD and BOD; water treatment. Air: greenhouse effect, ozone depletion, acid rain and photochemical smog. Soil: erosion, acidity and salinity. Mitigation: green chemistry, organic fertiliser, renewable energy, waste management and cleaner products.
