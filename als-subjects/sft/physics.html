Physics Notes

Measurement, forces, equilibrium, energy, rotational motion, electricity, heat, matter properties and fluids.

Syllabus-aligned study material based on the uploaded NIE Grade 12–13 Science for Technology syllabus. Sinhala explanations and further diagrams, examples and activities are being expanded chapter by chapter.
TOPIC 01

Measurement and errors

Physical quantities are expressed using numerical values and standard units. Prefixes represent powers of ten. Instruments have a least count and may have zero error.

Key notes

Fractional error = absolute error / measured value. Percentage error = fractional error × 100%. Practise Vernier caliper, micrometer screw gauge, travelling microscope, triple-beam balance and stopwatch readings.

TOPIC 02

Forces and Newton's laws

Force is a vector with magnitude, direction and point/line of action. Resolve forces into perpendicular components. Momentum is mass multiplied by velocity.

Key notes

p = mv; F = ma; weight W = mg. Newton's first law describes inertia; second law links resultant force and rate of change of momentum; third law describes action-reaction pairs. Friction: static, limiting and dynamic; F = μR.

TOPIC 03

Resultants, moments and equilibrium

Find resultants of collinear and inclined forces using vector methods and the parallelogram law. Moment is the turning effect of a force about a point.

Key notes

Moment = force × perpendicular distance. For equilibrium, resultant force and resultant moment are zero. Apply the syllabus cases for two/three forces and coplanar systems.

TOPIC 04

Work, energy and power

Work is energy transferred by a force through displacement. Mechanical energy includes kinetic and potential energy. In an ideal isolated system, total mechanical energy is conserved.

Key notes

W = Fs (in the force direction); translational kinetic energy = ½mv²; gravitational potential energy = mgh; power = work/time. Efficiency = useful output/input × 100%.

TOPIC 05

Rotational motion

Study angular displacement, angular velocity, angular acceleration and the relationship between linear and angular quantities. Apply torque and rotational equilibrium to practical systems.

Key notes

Relate tangential speed to angular speed with v = rω. Use the exact rotational formulae and sign conventions taught in the syllabus when solving problems.

TOPIC 06

Electricity and circuits

Study electric charge, current, potential difference, resistance, electrical energy and power; apply circuit laws to practical electrical appliances and circuit design.

Key notes

Use V = IR, P = VI, E = Pt and series/parallel resistance rules where applicable. Draw circuit diagrams with standard symbols and show units in every calculation.

TOPIC 07

Heat and thermal properties

Cover expansion of solids and liquids, heat exchange, specific heat capacity, changes of state and latent heat. Heat transfer occurs by conduction, convection and radiation.

Key notes

Q = mcΔT; Q = mL; heat capacity C = Q/ΔT. Distinguish latent heat from temperature change. Explain factors affecting conduction, convection and radiation.

TOPIC 08

Mechanical properties and fluids

Study mechanical properties of matter and fluid behaviour at rest and in motion. The syllabus includes density, relative density, hydrostatic pressure, Pascal's principle, upthrust and Archimedes' principle.

Key notes

Density ρ = m/V; pressure p = F/A; hydrostatic pressure p = ρgh. Explain buoyancy, centre of buoyancy and conditions for floating.
